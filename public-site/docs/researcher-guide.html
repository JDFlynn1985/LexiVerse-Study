Researcher's Guide: Best Practices

Welcome, Scholar. This guide is designed to help you leverage the full potential of LexiVerse Explorer for your biblical and theological research.

The Writing Hub & SBL Standards

LexiVerse is pre-configured for the Society of Biblical Literature (SBL) style guide. Use "Refine Tone" to convert informal notes into rigorous academic prose.

Local-First Research (RAG)

Your private library remains entirely in your browser. When you use the AI Study Assistant, the engine searches these papers for relevant context without data leaving your device.

Visual Theology Mapping

The Theology Map represents "Theological Weight" over time. Use the scatter chart to visualize density across historical eras (Patristic, Medieval, Reformation, Modern).
