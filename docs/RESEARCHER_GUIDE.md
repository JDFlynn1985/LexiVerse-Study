# LexiVerse Researcher's Guide: Best Practices

Welcome, Scholar. This guide is designed to help you leverage the full potential of LexiVerse Explorer for your biblical and theological research.

---

## ✍️ The Writing Hub & SBL Standards

LexiVerse is pre-configured for the **Society of Biblical Literature (SBL)** style guide.

### Tone Refinement
Use the "Refine Tone" feature to convert pastoral or informal notes into academically rigorous prose. The AI is instructed to preserve Greek and Hebrew terms while enhancing your scholarly vocabulary.

### The Integrity Scanner
Before submitting a paper, run the "Integrity Scan." The system checks your text against common theological definitions. 
- **Note**: This tool identifies "Scholarly Echoes"—phrases that closely resemble standard commentaries. It will suggest a citation (e.g., JFB or BDAG) to help you avoid accidental plagiarism.

---

## 📚 Local-First Research (RAG)

Your private library of research papers is your most valuable asset in LexiVerse.

### Grounding the AI
When you upload a paper to the **Library Hub**, it remains entirely in your browser. When you use the **AI Study Assistant**, the engine searches these papers for relevant context.
- **Pro-Tip**: Use descriptive filenames (e.g., `Pauline_Justification_Thesis.pdf`). The AI uses the filename in its citations: `[Ref: Pauline_Justification_Thesis.pdf]`.

### Managing Character Limits
The RAG engine works best with focused papers. If a document is over 100 pages, the keyword ranking might be diluted. Consider uploading specific chapters or excerpts for higher precision.

## 🗺️ Visual Theology & Geograpy

### The Theology Map
The density map (scatter chart) represents the "Theological Weight" of a concept over time. 
- **X-Axis**: Represents the chronological progression from the Patristic era to the Modern day.
- **Y-Axis / Density**: Represents how much that era influenced the current understanding of the term.

### Spatial Narrative (Geography Hub)
When analyzing a location like "Jericho," use the **Spatial Data** button. This opens the exact coordinates in Google Maps, allowing you to visualize the terrain described in the biblical narrative.

## 💬 Scholarly Discourse

### Institutional Seminars
If you are part of a registered seminary, toggle the **Institutional** mode in the Chat Hub. This provides a private channel for you and your colleagues to discuss coursework and research without global exposure.

### Direct Messaging
Need to collaborate privately? Click a scholar's avatar in the Chat Hub or search the registry in the **Direct Message Hub** to initiate a secure, peer-to-peer dialogue.
