Institutional Governance Manual

LexiVerse provides robust oversight tools for seminary administrators to maintain scholarly integrity and platform stability.

Role-Based Access Control (RBAC)

  • isAdmin: Grants access to system controls and institutional registry.
  • isModerator: Grants access to Wiki Peer-Review and DMCA Resolution.

Wiki Moderation Workflow

  1. Proposal: Researcher submits a draft with SBL works cited.
  2. Review: Moderators audit for academic rigor.
  3. Action: Approval triggers global publication and user notification.

DMCA & Automated Takedown

Upon submission of a formal complaint, flagged content (Wiki or Chat) is instantly hidden pending administrative investigation.
