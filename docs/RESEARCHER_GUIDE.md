
# LexiVerse Researcher's Guide: Best Practices

Welcome, Scholar. This guide is designed to help you leverage the full potential of LexiVerse Explorer for your biblical and theological research.

---

## ✍️ The Writing Hub & SBL Standards

LexiVerse is pre-configured for the **Society of Biblical Literature (SBL)** style guide.

### Citation Scanning
If you have a rough draft with scattered citations, use the **Citation Scanner**. It identifies raw references and generates a structured SBL bibliography that can be copied or exported as BibTeX.

### Tone Refinement
Use the "Refine Tone" feature to convert pastoral or informal notes into academically rigorous prose. The AI is instructed to preserve Greek and Hebrew terms while enhancing your scholarly vocabulary.

---

## 📚 Modern Bible Versions (Licensing)

Public domain texts (KJV, etc.) are available by default. For modern copyrighted translations:
1.  Navigate to the **Licensing Hub**.
2.  **Institutional Access**: If your seminary provides access, sign in via the **Institutional SSO** option to automatically unlock translations.
3.  **Individual License**: Enter a valid publisher-provided key to unlock versions like the NIV or ESV for your personal workspace.

---

## 🗺️ Visual Theology & Synoptics

### The Theology Map
The density map represents the "Theological Weight" of a concept over time. 
- **Influence Score**: Higher density indicates a period (e.g., Reformation) that heavily shaped the modern understanding of the term.

### Synoptic Aligner
When analyzing the Gospels, use the **Synoptic Aligner**. Enter an event (e.g., "The Transfiguration") to see a side-by-side comparison of how Matthew, Mark, Luke, and John emphasize unique theological nuances of the same event.

---

## 💬 Scholarly Discourse

### Institutional Seminars
If you are part of a registered seminary, toggle the **Institutional** mode in the Chat Hub. This provides a private channel for coursework and research without global exposure.

### Direct Messaging
Need to collaborate privately? Search for colleagues in the **Direct Message Hub** to initiate a secure dialogue. You will receive an automated in-app notification when a peer replies.
