# LexiVerse: Scholarly Style & Citation Standards

LexiVerse Explorer is engineered to uphold the highest standards of academic rigor in biblical and theological studies. This guide outlines how the platform handles linguistic data and citations.

## ✍️ The SBL Standard (2nd Edition)
The **Society of Biblical Literature (SBL) Handbook of Style** is the primary authority for the platform's AI reasoning and writing tools.

### 1. Linguistic Precision
- **Original Alphabets**: AI-synthesized reports (Assistant/Lexicon) MUST include Greek and Hebrew terms in their original characters (e.g., *λόγος*, *בראשית*).
- **Transliteration**: All original language terms are accompanied by a standard phonetic transliteration.
- **Morphology**: Morphological analysis follows the standard parsing conventions (Case, Number, Gender, Tense, Voice, Mood).

### 2. The "Academic Tone" Engine
When using the **Writing Hub's "Refine Tone"** feature, the AI applies the following heuristics:
- **Formality**: Strips second-person pronouns ("you") and informal contractions.
- **Vocabulary**: Enhances pastoral or colloquial phrasing with precise theological terminology (e.g., changing "Jesus died for us" to "the substitutionary atonement of Christ").
- **Objectivity**: Prioritizes nuanced, evidence-based claims over dogmatic assertions.

### 3. Citations & Bibliography
- **Primary Sources**: Bible references use standard SBL abbreviations (e.g., "1 Cor" instead of "First Corinthians").
- **Secondary Sources**: Bibliographies generated by the platform are formatted as **BibTeX** for Zotero/Mendeley or as standard SBL 2nd Edition bibliographic entries.
- **RAG Attribution**: Fragments from the researcher's local library are cited using the bracketed reference format: `[Ref: FileName.pdf]`.

## 🛡️ Academic Integrity
The **Integrity Scanner** identifies "Scholarly Echoes"—phrases that are common in standard commentaries like BDAG or JFB. The system will suggest a citation rather than just flagging plagiarism, aiding the student in proper attribution.
