# LexiVerse Explorer: Feature Catalog

LexiVerse Explorer is an integrated research environment designed for biblical scholars and theological researchers.

---

## 🤖 AI Research Hub (The Brain)

### 1. AI Study Assistant
Grounded synthesis that quotes primary scripture sources and your personal research library simultaneously.

### 2. Scholarly Dialogues
Simulate rigorous, respectful theological debates between historical figures (e.g., Augustine vs Pelagius).

### 3. Audio Exegesis Hub
Convert synthesized research reports into high-fidelity academic narration for auditory review.

### 4. Synoptic Aligner
Map narrative events across Matthew, Mark, Luke, and John to analyze theological emphasis.

---

## ✍️ Academic Synthesis (The Writing Hub)

- **Citation Scanner**: Automatically extract and structure SBL-style bibliographic data.
- **Tone Refinement**: Adjust drafts for academic, pastoral, or concise tones.
- **Integrity Scanner**: Scan text for uncredited scholarly echoes and suggest citations.
- **Cross-Ref Scanner**: Identify "covert" semantic links to scripture within your draft.

---

## 📚 Data & Workspace Management

- **Library Hub**: Manage network-isolated papers and semantic vector indices.
- **Zotero Sync**: Bidirectional synchronization with your Zotero collections.
- **Licensing Hub**: Unlock modern copyrighted translations via institutional SSO.
- **Archive Hub**: Restore and manage saved research sessions.

---

## 🛡️ Governance & Security

- **Age Verification (15+)**: Strict compliance with global data protection regulations.
- **Dual-Layer Auth**: Secure credential verification on both client and server.
- **System Control Panel**: Administrative management of AI providers and network modes.
