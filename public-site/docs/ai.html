The Research Brain

LexiVerse Explorer utilizes Firebase Genkit (1.x) as its primary orchestration layer, providing a unified interface for cloud-based synthesis and local-network inference.

Local RAG (Retrieval-Augmented Generation)

The Digital Library enables researchers to ground the AI in their personal research papers without data leaving the client.

  1. Local Indexing: Documents are parsed in the browser and stored in IndexedDB.
  2. Semantic Chunking: Text is split into fragments with overlap to preserve context.
  3. TF-IDF Ranking: Chunks are ranked locally based on weighted keyword frequency.
  4. Attributed Synthesis: AI is instructed to cite these fragments using the filename.

Multimodal Integration

The platform supports Voice transcription via Gemini Multimodal and Manuscript OCR via Gemini Vision, preserving Greek and Hebrew terms with high precision.
