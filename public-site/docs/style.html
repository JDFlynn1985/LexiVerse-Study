Scholarly Style & Citation Standards

LexiVerse Explorer is engineered to uphold the highest standards of academic rigor. This guide outlines how the platform handles linguistic data and citations.

The SBL Standard (2nd Edition)

The Society of Biblical Literature (SBL) Handbook of Style is the primary authority for the platform's AI reasoning and writing tools.

Linguistic Precision

  • Original Alphabets: AI-synthesized reports MUST include Greek and Hebrew terms in their original characters (e.g., λόγος, בראשית).
  • Morphology: Morphological analysis follows standard parsing conventions (Case, Number, Gender, Tense, etc.).

The "Academic Tone" Engine

When using the Writing Hub's "Refine Tone" feature, the AI applies strict heuristics for formality, objective vocabulary, and evidence-based claims.
